Our Story

Salisbury Transmission Specialists has been providing the best and widest range of automatic transmission services at 4 Arcoona Road, Salisbury Plain for over 4 decades. Thier experience and knowledge is unmatched in the transmission industry.

Owner, Simon has been repairing, rebuilding and servicing automatic transmissions for well over 30 years and has always taken pride in the quality of his work. Simon and his team understand the importance of honest advice and quality, reliable repairs.

Simon Says: "As a small family run business we take pride in helping our customers keep thier vehicles on the road in a friendly, honest and helpful manner. We encorage our customers to speak directly with the technician taking care of thier vehicle for total piece of mind. No mater the vehicle or problem we strive to provide old school, local service."

We specialise in automatic transmission repairs only. We do not work on Diff, Transfer Case, Manual Transmissions or other driveline parts.

Whether you're experiencing a transmission issue or simply need routine servicing, get in touch with our friendly team to discuss your vehicle.
