Welcome to Salisbury Transmissions

Locally and family owned for over 40 years, at Salisbury Trasmission Specialists we are your one stop shop for all your automatic transmission repairs and servicing. From basic fluid and filter changes to complete rebuilds, we have it all covered.

We service and repair all automatic transmissions, from 2 speed PowerGlides all the way to state of the art CVT and DCT transmissions. Our staff are fully accredited and qualified to work on all transmissions on the market, with unsurpassed knowledgeand old school friendly service we can get your transmission running right or your vehicle back on the road.
